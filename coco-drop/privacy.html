Coco Drop: Fruit Merge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026. This version takes effect on that date.

This policy explains what information the mobile game Coco Drop: Fruit Merge ("Coco Drop", "the game") for iPhone and Android collects, how it is used and shared, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. Coco Drop is made by Paulino Nogales, who publishes it under the name Proyecto Games ("we", "us"). You can reach us at proyectogames01@gmail.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. The game never asks for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. We run no servers, and we have no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device information, including identifiers, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, you are asked first, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Who we are

Coco Drop is developed by Paulino Nogales, an independent developer based in Spain, who publishes his games under the name Proyecto Games. For the personal information we handle ourselves, such as the emails you send us, Paulino Nogales is responsible (the "controller" under data protection laws such as the GDPR). Google is a separate, independent controller for the information its AdMob software collects in the game (section 3).

For anything about this policy or your information, write to proyectogames01@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

The game saves a few things in its own storage on your phone, so it can work:

  • your best score and the fruit you have discovered;
  • your settings: sound, music, volume, particles, screen shake and vibration;
  • the time the last full-screen ad was shown, so ads can be spaced out.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the game plays offline. Deleting the game deletes it. If your phone backs up its apps to your own iCloud or Google account, that backup may hold a copy; it stays in your account, and we cannot see it.

3. Ads by Google AdMob

The game is free because it shows ads: a small banner at the bottom of the screen while you play, and a full-screen ad between games, when you tap "Play again" or Restart. Full-screen ads never appear during a run or in the first 2 minutes after you open the game, and come at most once every 90 seconds. There are no rewarded ads.

The ads come from Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited if you are in the EEA or Switzerland, and by Google LLC elsewhere. To show ads, the AdMob software in the game collects the following and sends it to Google:

  • Identifiers: your phone's advertising identifier (on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; on Android, unless you have deleted it), and other identifiers for the app and the ad session;
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out. The game never asks for your precise location;
  • Device and app information, such as phone model, operating system, language and app version;
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • Diagnostics: performance and error information about the ads.

Identifiers and cookies. Google and its partners use these identifiers, and cookies or similar technologies (inside the ads, and in storage on your phone that the ad software uses, for example to remember your ad choices), to recognise your device when ads are shown.

Why. Google uses this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain and improve its services. If you agree (in the US, unless you opt out), it is also used to personalise ads to your interests.

Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your ad choices

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears when you first open the game and asks whether ads may use your data, for example to personalise them. You can accept, refuse, or choose purpose by purpose. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then Privacy, then Ad choices. Withdrawing does not affect what happened before.
  • In US states with privacy laws, such as California, you can opt out of the use of your data for targeted ads (its "sale" or "sharing") in the game: Settings, then Privacy, then Ad choices.
  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track, with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (under Google, then Ads, or Privacy, then Ads, depending on the phone).

The Ad choices row appears in the game only where Google requires it. Ads still appear when you limit the use of your data; they are just less tailored to you, and the game works the same.

5. What we receive

We receive no personal information from the game. We have no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own, and we never see the identifiers AdMob collects. The app stores and AdMob give us only reports that do not identify you, such as numbers of downloads, crash reports your phone shares with developers if you allowed it, and ad earnings.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and to deal with your request.

6. Who receives information

  • Google, through AdMob, as described in section 3. Advertisers and ad technology companies that work with Google may take part in showing and measuring ads, under Google's rules; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the consent form lists them.
  • Our email provider, Google (Gmail), which stores the emails you send us.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires us to hand over something we hold.

We share information with no one else. Google is bound by its agreements with us and by its own privacy policy to protect the information it receives, and gives it the same or equal protection as this policy describes.

7. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Saving your progress and settings on your phone is necessary to provide the game you chose to play.
  • Storing or reading identifiers on your phone for ads, and personalised ads, rely on your consent, given in Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time (section 4).
  • Non-personalised ads, measuring ads, limiting repeats and preventing fraud rely on legitimate interests: keeping the game free and the ads safe. The consent form shows where this applies and lets you object.
  • Answering your emails relies on our legitimate interest in replying to you and, for requests about your rights, on our legal obligations.

8. International transfers

Google may process the information AdMob collects, and the emails you send us, in the United States and other countries. For information from the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google relies on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, as Google describes in its data transfer frameworks.

9. How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the game. Deleting it deletes your best score, discovered fruit and settings, and we cannot restore them.
  • Ad information held by Google: for the periods Google sets out in How Google retains data we collect.
  • Emails you send us: only as long as we need them to answer you and deal with your request, and at most 12 months after our last message to each other, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Visits to this website: GitHub keeps its own logs (section 14).

10. Security

We collect as little as we can, which is the best protection. The game's saved data is kept in its own storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. The AdMob software sends information to Google over encrypted connections. Emails you send us are kept in our email account and used only to reply to you. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what we hold.

11. Your rights, and how to delete your information

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • correct it, or have it deleted;
  • restrict or object to how it is used, and take it with you (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what happened before;
  • opt out of targeted advertising, or of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

How to make a request

Write to us at proyectogames01@gmail.com and tell us what you would like. We reply within one month, and tell you if we need more time where the law allows it. We may ask you to confirm that the request comes from you, for example by replying from the same email address. Using your rights is free, and you will not be treated differently for using them.

How to delete information or withdraw consent

  • Saved on your phone: delete the game.
  • Used for ads: change or withdraw your choices as described in section 4. On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, so new ad data is not linked to the old; on iPhone, turning off tracking stops apps from getting it. We never see these identifiers, so we cannot look up or delete the data Google holds; the Google Privacy Policy explains how to make requests to Google directly.
  • Emails you sent us: ask us, and we will delete them.

Complaints

If you can, write to us first, so we can try to help. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority: in the EEA, the one where you live, work or think the problem happened (list of EEA authorities); in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. We are based in Spain, where the authority is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

12. Notice for US residents

This part is for residents of California and other US states with consumer privacy laws. It describes the personal information collected through the game, including in the last 12 months.

  • Categories collected: through Google AdMob, identifiers (such as the advertising identifier and the IP address); internet or other electronic network activity (device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics); and approximate location, worked out from the IP address. If you email us: your email address and what you write. No sensitive personal information and no precise location are collected.
  • Sources: your phone, through the AdMob software in the game; and you, when you write to us.
  • Purposes: showing and measuring ads, limiting repeats, preventing fraud and keeping ads secure, and, unless you opt out, targeted advertising. Emails are used only to answer you.
  • Disclosure, "sale" and "sharing": the information collected through AdMob goes to Google and, through Google, to advertisers and ad technology companies, for advertising. We never sell personal information for money, but under some state laws this may count as "selling" or "sharing" it for targeted advertising. You can opt out in the game: Settings, then Privacy, then Ad choices. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the personal information of anyone under 16.
  • Retention: as described in section 9.
  • Your rights: to know and access, correct and delete your personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing and of targeted advertising, without being treated differently for using these rights. Make a request as described in section 11; an authorised agent may also write for you, with your signed permission. If we turn down a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and if you are not satisfied with the result, you can contact your state's attorney general.
  • Do Not Track: this website does not track you, and the game is not a web browser, so browser signals such as Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control change nothing here. In the game, use Ad choices.

13. Children

Coco Drop is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
