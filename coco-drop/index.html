Questions

How do I play?

Drag to aim and let go to drop a fruit. When two equal fruits touch, they merge into the next fruit on the ladder. Grow them all the way to the coconut, and look out for the secret fruit. Keep the fruit below the red line: if one stays above it for 5 seconds, the run ends. The game is endless, so a run lasts as long as you can keep it up.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and the game plays offline. There are no in-app purchases.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone: your best score, the fruit you have discovered and your settings. Nothing is sent to us, so we can't move your progress to another phone or restore it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Why are there ads?

Ads keep the game free: a small banner at the bottom of the screen while you play, and sometimes a full-screen ad between games, when you tap "Play again" or Restart. Full-screen ads never interrupt a run, never appear in the first 2 minutes after you open the game, and come at most once every 90 seconds. There is no way to pay to remove them, as the game has no purchases.

How do I change my ad choices?

  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws (such as California): in the game, open Settings, then Privacy, then Ad choices. This row appears only where Google requires it.
  • On iPhone: in your phone's Settings, open Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • On Android: in your phone's Settings, under Google, then Ads (or Privacy, then Ads, depending on the phone).

The privacy policy explains these choices in full.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the game deletes everything it saved on your phone. To have emails you sent us deleted, or for any other privacy request, write to us. For ad data held by Google, see your rights and deletion.
